About Me

I'm a Senior Technical Writer with 9 years of experience, covering a broad spectrum of documentation, from API guides and developer walkthroughs to end-user guides, tutorials and release notes.

I work in Docs-as-Code environments (Markdown, Git, IntelliJ) and collaborate closely with Product, Engineering, QA, and UX/UI teams to make sure documentation helps both new users and those working through integration specifics. I also use AI tools like Claude Code and Gemini to accelerate research, drafting, and technical validation, without compromising quality.

Beyond writing itself, I believe documentation processes, guidelines and best practices keep a team's knowledge consistent and scalable. When I write, my goal is to create content that is accurate, easy to navigate, and useful long-term.

I'm bilingual in English (C1) and German (B2), and have worked across North American and European professional environments throughout my career.
