Onboarding via Self-Registration [WIP]

This guide explains how to create a new enterprise account in Banqup through self-registration.

Prerequisites​

No account is needed to start. You will need:

  • A valid company TRN (Tax Registration Number) or VAT number
  • Access to the email address you want to register with

Step 1: Start the onboarding process​

Go to the Banqup login page and click Create an account.

[Screenshot: Banqup login page with Create an account button]

  1. Select your language and country if needed, then click Create an account.

[Screenshot: Language and country selection]

note

The language and country are preselected based on your browser language and geolocation. You can change them if needed.

If you select The Netherlands, France, or Serbia, you will be asked to confirm a redirect to that country's platform.

Step 2: Enter your personal details​

You will arrive on the personal details page.

[Screenshot: Personal details form]

Strong authentication

At this step, you can choose to log in with a digital identity provider. Strong authentication is mandatory for Baltic countries (Latvia, Estonia, and Lithuania).

  1. Fill in the personal details form.
  2. Click Continue.

Step 3: Verify your email​

  1. Check your inbox for a verification code.
  2. Enter the code on the verification screen.
  3. Click Continue.

[Screenshot: Email verification screen]

Step 4: Find your company​

On the next screen, search for your company. You can search by:

  • Company name
  • VAT number (Belgium only)
  • TRN

[Screenshot: Company search screen]

note
  • You can change the language and country in the top right corner if needed.
  • You can sign out and return to complete the onboarding later.
  • If you are a sole trader or don't have a CBE/VAT number, select the sole trader entity type instead.

If you searched by company name: select your company from the results and click This is my business.

[Screenshot: Company search results]

If you searched by TRN or VAT, or if there is only one result, you will be taken directly to the company details screen.

Step 5: Confirm company details​

Review the pre-filled company details and click Continue.

[Screenshot: Company details screen]

TRN validation
  • The TRN number is mandatory for all countries. German companies also require the District court name.
  • The system checks whether the TRN already exists and whether the format is correct for the selected country.
  • For Baltic countries, the TRN field is locked — it is verified automatically using your Personal ID.
  • For Belgian companies searching by TRN, the VAT is prefilled with the BE prefix. If your company is not VAT liable, delete the VAT number manually.
  • In Singapore, test enterprises can be added to the production environment using the SGTST prefix before the UEN number, for example SGTSTT08GA0028A.

Step 6: Handle duplicate TRN (if applicable)​

If the TRN already exists in the system, the following applies:

If this is not the first time you are registering this enterprise: you will see an alert. You can still add the company as a subsidiary by clicking Add it again.

[Screenshot: Duplicate TRN alert modal]

Each enterprise registered for the first time receives client alias 0001. Subsequent registrations of the same TRN receive 0002, 0003, and so on. The alias combined with the TRN creates a unique identifier for each enterprise.

The first registration is set as Default/Headquarters. Additional registrations are treated as subsidiaries. You can change a subsidiary to a default enterprise at any time.

[Screenshot: Company Information page showing entity type and alias]

If you are registering via an accountant invitation and the TRN you enter differs from the one in the accountant's Console, you will be asked to confirm that you want to accept the invitation.

note

This applies only when:

  • The invitation was sent to an email address with no existing account
  • The TRN entered already exists in the system

Step 7: Activate your subscription​

You will automatically receive a trial subscription valid for one month.

[Screenshot: Trial subscription confirmation screen]

You will also receive a confirmation email once your enterprise is activated.

To upgrade to a full subscription, go to the Subscriptions page.

Trial subscription exceptions
  • The trial subscription does not apply to Banqup Netherlands.
  • For Dutch companies, an enterprise approval task is triggered in the Coala app after onboarding. Once approved, you can access Banqup and activate a subscription. No default subscription is applied for Dutch companies.

Next steps​

Once your account is activated, you can:

  • Set up your company profile under Settings → Company Information
  • Activate the apps and features included in your subscription
  • Invite team members and configure user roles
