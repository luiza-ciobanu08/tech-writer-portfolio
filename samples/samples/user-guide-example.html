Import Sales Invoices

This guide explains how to import sales invoices into the Accounts Receivable (AR) workspace in Banqup and track their status after import.

Before you start​

Make sure you have the following:

  • The Send invoices and view sent invoices role activated on your account
  • A document in one of the supported formats: PDF, UBL, JPG, or PNG
note

Banqup automatically converts PDFs and images to UBL format and stores both versions. You can import invoices, credit notes, debit notes, and expenses.

Due dates

Every imported document receives a due date. If no due date is found during processing, the default payment date is applied. To configure this, go to Settings → Outbound → Default payment after.

Import a document manually​

Use this method to upload a document directly from your computer.

Before you start, confirm that the Upload setting is activated under Settings → Outbound → Import Sales Invoices.

  1. Open the AR workspace.
  2. Click Upload documents.

  1. Select your file and confirm the upload.

Alternative: Drag and drop the file directly onto the document list.

Result: The document appears in your AR list. The upload is recorded in the Events section.

Import a document via email​

Use this method to send invoices created outside Banqup directly into your AR list.

  1. Locate the Banqup-provided email address and subject line in Settings > Outbound > Email addresses.

  1. Send your invoice to that email address using the exact subject line provided.

Result: The document is automatically imported into your AR list.

Use the provided email address and subject line

If you send the email from a different address or use a different subject line, the document will not be imported.

Exception: Invoices from whitelisted senders do not need the unique subject line.

Email imports are recorded in the Events section.

Import documents via HTTP connector​

Use this method to connect third-party software — such as accounting tools — to deliver documents automatically into AR via API.

  1. Go to Settings → Outbound → Import sales invoices and activate Connect your invoicing software.

  1. Copy the unique authentication key displayed in this section.
  2. Share the key with the sender.
  3. The sender uses these credentials to authenticate and deliver documents via the Universal Connector API.

Result: Documents sent via the connector appear automatically in your AR list.

Track your imported documents​

Regardless of how a document was imported, you can track its status on the Received files page.

The page shows information such as:

  • Sender — who submitted the document
  • Status — current processing state
  • Import method — how the document was delivered
